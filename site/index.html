Shoot no shoot training, in your browser.

Hold Fire is a web-based app for practicing the split-second judgment behind every trigger press. Watch a scenario unfold, decide whether to shoot or hold fire, and see what you got right and what you missed.

Get in touch Follow development

What it is

Firearms simulators that teach use of force decision training have mostly lived in agency training rooms, behind expensive hardware. Hold Fire brings the core drill to any modern browser: a scenario plays, a threat may or may not appear, and you have to make the call. Every decision is scored for correctness and reaction time, with a debrief on the cues that mattered.

Who it's for

  • Concealed carry holders and armed citizens who want to rehearse threat recognition between range trips.
  • Firearms instructors looking for a shoot don't shoot drill they can assign as homework.
  • Security officers and small departments that need affordable judgmental use of force practice.
  • Competitive and recreational shooters who want to sharpen decision speed, not just accuracy.

Where things stand

Hold Fire is in early development. The core is source-available on GitHub under the Business Source License 1.1. If you want to be told when it launches, suggest a scenario, or talk about using it in your training program, open an issue on GitHub.

Training aid only. Hold Fire is meant to supplement, not replace, qualified instruction and live-fire training. It is not legal advice, and use of force laws vary by jurisdiction.
